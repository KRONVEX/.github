# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

As maintainers and contributors of **KRONVEX**, we are committed to maintaining an open, rigorous, respectful, and harassment-free engineering environment for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively, directly, and respectfully in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Critique ideas, architectures, and code — never people.
- Share context clearly and collaborate in good faith.
- Respect project maintainers' time, security boundaries, and decision-making processes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Submitting deliberately malicious code, spam, or unverified automated PRs.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to **kronvex1@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers reserve the right to remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
